# Schengen individual tourism from mainland China / 中国大陆申请申根个人旅游短期签证

Module ID: `schengen-tourism-china`
Maturity: `M1 OFFICIAL_REVIEWED` for the EU common rules, the China harmonised evidence list, and the six destination entry points in [the source registry](schengen-tourism-china-sources.md). No live portal or applicant has been tested. Last verified: 2026-09-26.

## Trigger and route / 适用与路由

Use for a mainland Chinese ordinary-passport holder resident in mainland China who seeks a short-stay Type C visa for **individual tourism**, normally at most 90 days in any 180-day period. First determine the competent state: sole destination; otherwise the main destination by purpose or longest stay; for equal stays without a distinct main destination, first Schengen entry. Verify the consular district for the applicant's residence. Do not choose a country merely because appointments are easier.

This module covers common rules for all Schengen states. It has checked country entry points for **France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, and Switzerland**. For another Schengen country, use the EU country directory to find its official mission and label the local route `CASE_RESEARCH` until its China instructions are verified. The six entry points are starting points, not interchangeable checklists.

Exclude ADS group tourism, family/friend invitation as the main purpose, business, paid work, study, medical treatment, airport transit, national long-stay visas, EU/EEA/Swiss family facilitation, non-mainland applications, and non-ordinary passports. If the authority requests a formal interview for this individual case, stop this module's automated execution and pass that step to the applicant or qualified human adviser. A normal centre visit, fingerprints, or routine questions while lodging documents is not automatically a formal interview.

## Review gates / 审核关卡

1. Ask for passport/residence, all destinations and nights, main purpose, dates and prior Schengen days, travel companions and minors, work/study status, budget and payer, prior refusals, and documents already held. Apply the [shared nine gates](../online-visitor-visa-workflow.md).
2. Check the EU competent-state and consular-district rule. Calculate planned Schengen days in the rolling 180-day window; check passport issue date, expiry, blank pages, and the destination's exact instructions. The EU overview says apply no earlier than six months and at least 15 days before travel; allow more time for appointments and possible extended review.
3. Refresh the [EU overview, China harmonised list, and destination mission](schengen-tourism-china-sources.md). The destination's current tourist checklist controls practical language, copies, signatures, centre, fees and submission method. Record a source and verification date for every material rule.
4. Check the official review questions: genuine tourist purpose and itinerary; sufficient means; accommodation; travel medical insurance valid across Schengen with at least EUR 30,000 cover; intention to leave; and consistency between all reservations, employer/leave, bank movements and form answers.
5. Build the China evidence matrix from the 2024 EU harmonised list plus destination checklist. Common items include used hukou pages (the EU list says no translation), recent three-month current-account statements rather than a deposit-only account, employment evidence with required English translation when applicable, round-trip flight reservation, whole-stay accommodation and travel plan. Minors, retirees, self-employed or unsupported applicants need profile-specific evidence. Do not generalise one state's extra document requirement to all.
6. Review evidence and correct conflicts. Flag wrong competent state, wrong tourist purpose, missing insurance/itinerary, inconsistent dates, false or cancelled reservations, missing translation, unexplained relied-upon funds, or incomplete minor permission. Do not suggest buying non-refundable travel to create evidence.
7. Review the complete form and pack with the applicant. Obtain immediate approval before online form submission, appointment, upload, payment or disclosure. The applicant signs and lodges originals/copies and gives biometrics at the responsible centre when instructed.
8. Track the receipt and official messages. If refused, read the reason and the **issuing state's** appeal instructions and deadline; do not assume one common Schengen appeal deadline. If granted, check name, validity, permitted duration, number of entries and itinerary fit before travel.

## Destination routing / 目的地入口

| Main destination | Official entry point | Case-specific check |
|---|---|---|
| France | France-Visas China → TLScontact | online visa wizard and generated checklist; centre and consular district |
| Germany | German Missions in China → VIDEX/VFS | tourist checklist, printed signed form and declarations; residence district |

See the exact URLs and review triggers in [the source registry](schengen-tourism-china-sources.md). Ireland, Cyprus and other non-Schengen European routes are not covered by this module.

## File and interview controls / 文件与面试边界

Name files descriptively in the private case record; no universal EU filename format exists. The local centre's upload or paper-pack instructions prevail. Keep original-language documents, translations, reservations and dates paired. If a formal interview notice arrives, record the notice and due date, pause the automated path, and hand the interview to the applicant. Resume case tracking only after the applicant reports the outcome and any further written request.
