# Canada visitor visa from mainland China / 中国大陆申请加拿大访客签证

Module ID: `canada-visitor-china`
Maturity: `M1 OFFICIAL_REVIEWED` — no observed applicant, live portal test, or completed individual dry run
Last verified: 2026-09-26; refresh before each live case and submission.

## Trigger and exclusion / 适用与排除

Use for a mainland Chinese ordinary-passport holder residing and applying from mainland China, seeking a temporary tourist or family/friend visit through an IRCC online visitor-visa route. Confirm that the applicant needs a visitor visa and choose the current IRCC portal version from its eligibility questions. Business visitor, super visa, study, work, transit, refugee travel document, complex inadmissibility, or a request for an interview exits this baseline. Biometrics and later passport submission may require applicant action in person.

## Intake and review / 建档与审核

Ask purpose and dates; passport validity; travel companions and minors; current job/study/business and home ties; trip budget, income and sponsor; accommodation or invitation; past travel and immigration history; health or criminal/immigration issues; existing Canadian biometrics; document languages. The officer assesses whether the person will leave Canada after the visit, has enough money, has ties outside Canada, and is admissible. Never invent a fixed minimum balance or approval probability. A Canadian invitation does not remove the need to prove temporary intent.

## End-to-end route / 完整路径

1. Apply the [shared nine gates](../online-visitor-visa-workflow.md); confirm visitor purpose and online route.
2. Refresh [official sources](canada-visitor-china-sources.md), country-specific visa-office instructions, current portal checklist, fee, biometrics status, and processing time.
3. Build a tailored evidence matrix. Identity and current portal checklist are the hard baseline; employment, finances, travel history, itinerary, invitation, and minor consent depend on circumstances and the generated checklist.
4. Draft answers from verified facts. Cross-check job dates, available funds, who pays, host relationship, visit length, and prior refusals. Resolve discrepancies truthfully.
5. Translate relied-upon non-English/French documents under current IRCC instructions. The visitor guide calls for an English/French translation and an affidavit when the translator is not certified; applicants or their family must not translate. Check whether the current portal/local checklist calls for original or certified copy details.
6. Review the exact digital package with the applicant. Obtain immediate confirmation for upload, submission, and payment. Do not use a stored blanket authorization.
7. After submission, wait for the biometric instruction letter if biometrics are needed, then the applicant books and attends the collection site. Track any request for more information, medical exam, police certificate, or interview. An interview request exits the automated path.
8. If approved, follow IRCC's passport submission letter and verify the visa is placed in the passport before travel. If refused, map each written reason to facts and evidence before considering a new application.

## Pitfalls and controls / 踩坑与控制

- `BLOCKER`: current portal checklist item missing, false statement, conflicting employment/funding story, or untranslated relied-upon document.
- `WARNING`: recent unexplained deposits, weak explanation of return ties, unrealistic budget, expired/expiring passport or biometrics, or assumption that online approval alone permits travel before passport visa issuance.
- `ADVISORY`: name files descriptively (`Category_Applicant_Date_Language.ext`); this is internal practice, not an IRCC naming rule. Do not upload redundant personal data.
- Keep the biometrics letter deadline and appointment evidence in the case. Current official guidance says to act promptly; read the actual letter because individual instructions govern.
