# Saudi tourist eVisa / 沙特旅游电子签（中国大陆申请）

Module ID: `saudi-tourist-evisa-china` · Maturity: `M1 OFFICIAL_REVIEWED` · Verified: 2026-09-26. No real account or paid application tested.

Scope: mainland Chinese ordinary-passport holder applying from China for tourism. Umrah rules and seasonal/restricted-area notices require separate live checking; work, Hajj, residence, or business outside tourist scope is excluded. A formal individual interview request stops this path.

The [Saudi Ministry of Tourism eVisa terms](https://visa.visitsaudi.com/Home/TermsConditions) list China, require passport validity of at least six months from entry, and provide electronic issuance by email. The eVisa is for tourism or Umrah, **not work**. The official terms state one-year validity/multiple entry/maximum stay of three months unless the issued visa says otherwise; read the actual visa. Fees are dynamic and non-refundable, so do not hard-code a price. Border entry and biometric capture remain authority decisions.

Run the [shared nine gates](../online-visitor-visa-workflow.md): verify current eligibility and travel dates; build a small matrix of passport, identity, photo and portal prompts, itinerary/accommodation/funding where requested; compare spelling, passport expiry and actual purpose; show all draft answers and cost; seek immediate approval separately for upload, submission and payment; track email result; verify the issued visa and same-passport travel. For a minor, confirm the applying adult has authority. Do not ask for full payment credentials or submit in a sandbox.

`BLOCKER`: under-six-month passport, work/Hajj misrouted as tourism, false identity, a required portal field missing. `WARNING`: fee/insurance/seasonal rules may change; eVisa does not guarantee entry. `ADVISORY`: filenames are internal only. Reopen official terms and live form before any case or payment.

## 中文执行摘要

中国普通护照可在官方电子签渠道申请符合条件的旅游访问，但赴麦加等季节性限制和朝觐目的必须另查，旅游签不允许工作。先核实护照在入境时至少还有六个月有效期，再按实时表单检查身份、照片、用途及条件材料；申请人审阅后分别确认上传、提交和付款。费用与保险等动态项目以当期官方页面为准；电子签不保证入境，正式面试通知会中止此模块。
