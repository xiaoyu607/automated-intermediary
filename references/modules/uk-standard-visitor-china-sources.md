## Community signals reviewed

Applicant reports and forum discussions repeatedly mentioned unexplained third-party transfers, large deposits, sponsor-only cases, weak home ties, and inconsistencies. These were used only to identify candidate pitfalls. The module promotes them to controls only where UK-S1, UK-S4, or UK-S6 independently supports the underlying concern.

No forum, social post, video, or intermediary article is treated as a mandatory rule.
