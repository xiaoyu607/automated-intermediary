# Australia Visitor 600 Tourist stream from mainland China / 中国大陆申请澳大利亚600旅游访客签证

Module ID: `australia-visitor-600-china`
Maturity: `M1 OFFICIAL_REVIEWED` — no observed applicant, live portal test, or completed individual dry run
Last verified: 2026-09-26; refresh before each live case and submission.

## Trigger and exclusion / 适用与排除

Use for a mainland Chinese ordinary-passport holder applying online from mainland China for the **subclass 600 Tourist stream (apply outside Australia)**, for tourism or visiting family/friends. Confirm the applicant will be outside Australia when applying and when a decision is made. Business Visitor, Sponsored Family, medical, work, study as main purpose, onshore application, and interview-required cases exit this baseline. The stream does not permit work.

## Intake and review / 建档与审核

Ask actual purpose, dates, passport, current location, employment/study/business, family and financial ties, total cost, payer, invitation/accommodation, companions/minors, past visas/refusals, health and character issues, and document languages. The authority assesses a genuine temporary visit and sufficient funds for stay and departure, with health and character requirements. Do not assert a universal bank-balance threshold or promised grant period.

## End-to-end route / 完整路径

1. Apply the [shared nine gates](../online-visitor-visa-workflow.md); verify the Tourist stream and offshore position.
2. Refresh [official sources](australia-visitor-600-china-sources.md), ImmiAccount instructions, current fee and processing estimate, and any China biometrics arrangements.
3. Create a case-specific evidence matrix from the live application checklist: identity, visit purpose, available funds, home circumstances, sponsorship/invitation when relevant, minor consent, and prior history. Distinguish required prompts from helpful evidence.
4. Draft and audit all answers against evidence. Check the travel budget, employment dates, payer, intended activity, dependants, and previous immigration outcomes. Correct errors honestly.
5. Translate all non-English documents into English; include source and translation. For a translator outside Australia, record full name, address, telephone, qualifications, and language experience in English on each translation. Use clear colour scans; keep all pages of one document together.
6. Review the exact ImmiAccount application and attachments with the applicant; obtain separate immediate approval for upload, submission, and fee payment. Government processing begins after payment.
7. Track ImmiAccount and reply to biometrics, health, additional-document, or correction requests. The applicant completes personal steps. Stop Skill execution for an interview request.
8. Read the written grant or refusal. If granted, record visa dates, entry conditions, stay limit and travel-document linkage; if refused, record reasons and any stated review right before deciding next action.

## Pitfalls and controls / 踩坑与控制

- `BLOCKER`: applying to the wrong stream, intending work on Tourist stream, false/conflicting information, missing portal-required evidence, or unaddressed translation.
- `WARNING`: assuming biometrics or health checks are automatic or never needed, non-English material without the required translator details, unexplained funds, or travel arranged before written grant.
- `ADVISORY`: internal filename pattern `Category_Applicant_Date_Language.ext`; the live ImmiAccount constraints govern actual upload format and size.
- Use the **offshore** Tourist page; the similarly named onshore page has different location rules.
