# Kenya tourist eTA / 肯尼亚旅游电子旅行许可（中国大陆申请）

Module ID: `kenya-tourist-eta-china` · Maturity: `M1 OFFICIAL_REVIEWED` for route and official document classes, **not** a paid portal run · Verified: 2026-09-26.

Scope: mainland Chinese **ordinary** passport holder applying online from China for tourism. The [official Immigration eTA guide](https://etakenya.go.ke/how-to-apply) lists exemptions for Chinese diplomatic, official or service passports, not ordinary passports; nevertheless confirm the current ordinary-passport selector before creating an application. Work, residence, special exemption, and formal individual interview routes are excluded.

The [official guide](https://etakenya.go.ke/how-to-apply) requires an approved eTA before travel and lists passport validity of at least six months with a blank page, photo/selfie, contact details, arrival/departure itinerary, accommodation and payment method. The live [eligibility page](https://etakenya.go.ke/eligibility) determines product and fee; do not promise a fixed charge, processing time, stay length or entry grant from a static screenshot.

Run the [shared nine gates](../online-visitor-visa-workflow.md): verify nationality/exemption/product; make a matrix for passport, photo, itinerary, accommodation, contacts and any live conditional documents; check identity and dates against source evidence; review exact declarations and cost; obtain separate immediate approval for upload, submission and payment; track eTA result and prepare the applicant for border inspection. Do not enter real data or pay in a sandbox.

`BLOCKER`: assuming Chinese ordinary passports share the diplomatic exemption, missing required travel/accommodation data, expired passport, conflicting identity. `WARNING`: dynamic product, fee and exemption list. `ADVISORY`: any suggested filename is an internal convention only. Reopen both official pages before a live case.

## 中文执行摘要

肯尼亚官方 ETA 豁免清单中提到的是中国外交、公务等特殊护照，不能套用于普通护照。普通护照游客先核对实时资格选择器，再审查至少六个月有效且有空白页的护照、照片、联系方式、往返行程、住宿及系统条件材料。申请人审阅后分别确认上传、提交和付款；电子许可仍不保证边境准入。
