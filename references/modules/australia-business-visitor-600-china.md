# Australia Visitor 600 Business Visitor stream / 澳大利亚 600 商务访客签证

Module ID: `australia-business-visitor-600-china` · Maturity: `M2 DRY_RUN` **only for the fictional employed-adult conference/contract-meeting profile in [the sandbox](../evaluations/australia-business-visitor-sandbox-2026-09-26.md)**; all other profiles are at most `M1 OFFICIAL_REVIEWED`. Official-source review: 2026-09-26. No live portal run, real applicant, payment or grant.

## Trigger and exits / 适用与退出

Mainland Chinese ordinary-passport holder resident in China, applying online from China while **outside Australia**, and remaining outside Australia until decision; actual purpose is permitted short business visitor activity such as unpaid conference attendance, general business enquiries or contract negotiation. Check whether an APEC Business Travel Card already grants relevant travel rights. Do not use for providing services to an Australian business, paid participation by conference organisers, direct sales to the public, employment, study as primary purpose, onshore applications, or family members assumed to be included. A separate formal interview request pauses execution. The government decides visa conditions and stay; do not promise a fixed grant period.

## Intake / 建档问题

Ask the exact planned activity and who benefits/pays; conference or host details; dates; Australian business contacts; employer and current role; ABTC or existing Australian visa; passport, residence and location at application/decision; travel companions; funding and return plan; prior visas/refusals; health/character flags; document languages. Questions about “business” must distinguish negotiation/meetings from hands-on work or service delivery.

## Nine-gate execution / 九关执行

1. **Intake/route:** select Business Visitor stream, not Tourist stream, work visa, ADS stream or paper Form 1415. Confirm offshore status and permitted activity using [official sources](australia-business-visitor-600-china-sources.md).
2. **Eligibility:** test genuine temporary visit, funds, health/character and any ABTC alternative. A paid speaker, on-site service delivery or public sales is a category blocker; do not merely reword the activity.
3. **Current rules:** reopen the official stream and ImmiAccount; record current charge, processing guide, personal-step requests and file rules. Treat current fee and timing as dynamic.
4. **Evidence matrix:** use the live application checklist. Identity/passport and visit purpose are baseline; invitation, conference registration, employer letter, business contacts, company activity, prior commercial contact, income/funds and return ties are case-dependent official examples. Health, police, minor or name-change records are conditional. Do not request a “universal” bank balance or every optional document.
5. **Audit:** compare form, invitation, employer letter, itinerary, dates, organiser payment, work description, funds and previous outcomes. Check evidence provenance and whether any claimed meeting is real.
6. **Translation/correction:** supply English translations plus originals for non-English documents. Translators outside Australia need not be accredited, but each translation must include their full name, address, phone and relevant language qualifications/experience in English. Clear colour scans; keep each multi-page document together. Do not fabricate a corrected invitation or translate selectively.
7. **Final review:** applicant checks exact ImmiAccount answers, evidence, declarations, current charge, outstanding warnings and any agent/representation issue. No known blocker and completed applicant review are needed before `READY_TO_SUBMIT`.
8. **Action:** require separate immediate consent before upload, submission, payment, booking or disclosure. The applicant personally completes requested biometrics/health steps; formal interview notice exits this module. Do not use paper Form 1415 unless Home Affairs explicitly instructs that route in writing.
9. **Outcome:** track ImmiAccount and official messages. Record written grant conditions, entry count, stay or refusal reasons; no travel booking assumption before written grant. Refresh rule changes and rerun affected tests.

## Risks and boundaries / 风险与边界

- `BLOCKER`: actual work mislabelled as business visit; applicant in Australia at application or decision; false host/employer statement; identity or date conflict; required evidence/translation missing.
- `WARNING`: invitation alone is not proof of genuine visit; ABTC or existing visa overlooked; biometrics/health request assumed automatic or impossible; fee and file limits copied from an old screenshot.
- `ADVISORY`: `Category_Applicant_Date_Language.ext` is a filing convention, not an official filename rule.
- This is an organizational tool, not an appointed Australian migration agent or legal practitioner. The [official assistance rules](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/help-support/who-can-help-with-your-application/using-a-legal-practitioner) govern any regulated representation. If legal strategy or representation is needed, refer to a qualified person; the applicant remains responsible for facts and declarations.
