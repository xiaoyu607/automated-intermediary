# Sri Lanka tourist ETA / 斯里兰卡旅游 ETA（中国大陆申请）

Module ID: `sri-lanka-tourist-eta-china` · Maturity: `M1 OFFICIAL_REVIEWED` · Verified: 2026-09-26. No real application or payment tested.

Scope: mainland Chinese ordinary-passport holder applying online from China for a short tourism visit. Business, transit, residence, and formal-interview cases need other rules. Confirm current China nationality selection and fee/waiver in the live [official ETA portal](https://eta.gov.lk/slvisa/visainfo/center.jsp?locale=en_US); a general free-ETA announcement does not prove China is in the free cohort.

The [official application instructions](https://eta.gov.lk/slvisa/visainfo/apply.jsp?locale=en_US) allow self-service online submission and describe acknowledgement, approval or referral, status checking, non-refund, and entry evidence: passport valid at least six months from arrival, confirmed return ticket and sufficient funds. A referral notice is **not** approval; the applicant must follow authority instructions. The portal says ETA approval does not itself guarantee entry.

Follow the [shared nine gates](../online-visitor-visa-workflow.md). Matrix passport and identity, travel purpose/dates, return ticket, funds, contact details and live form prompts. Audit spelling, passport validity, return timing and ability to pay. Show exact declarations and any fee; obtain immediate user confirmation for submission and payment. Track acknowledgement to decision; do not mark `APPROVED` on acknowledgement or referral. At arrival, applicant carries the approval and evidence. Pause on an individual interview request.

`BLOCKER`: invalid passport, wrong purpose, inconsistent identity, missing return/funds, or treating referral as approval. `WARNING`: China fee and scheme terms are dynamic; recheck before payment. `ADVISORY`: internal filename suggestions are not official requirements.

## 中文执行摘要

斯里兰卡短期旅游使用官方 ETA 渠道；中国护照是否享有当期免费政策，必须看实时国籍选择器，不从一般公告推断。检查护照有效期、返程票、资金与身份一致性；申请人分别确认提交和付款。系统回执或转介通知都不等于批准，只有正式批准后才能按批准条件准备出行；正式面试通知会暂停自动流程。
