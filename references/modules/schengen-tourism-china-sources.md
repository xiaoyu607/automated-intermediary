For any of the other Schengen states, [the Commission's country directory](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/schengen/visa-policy/applying-schengen-visa_en) is a route-finding source, not a local checklist. Verify its official China mission and mark that branch `CASE_RESEARCH` until completed.

Volatile checks: destination jurisdiction, consular district, document language/translation, appointment operator, application form version, visa and service fee, centre inventory, biometrics reuse, processing time, and post-refusal instructions. The Italian checklist file is older; treat an unverified live checklist as `UNKNOWN`, not as a confirmed current requirement.
