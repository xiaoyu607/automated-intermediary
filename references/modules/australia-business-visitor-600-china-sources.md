# Australia subclass 600 Business Visitor — official source register / 澳大利亚商务访客官方来源

Verified 2026-09-26 for a mainland Chinese ordinary-passport holder applying from mainland China while outside Australia. Reopen all dynamic pages before a live case and before submission. The official page does not establish that no individual case can ever require an interview; an interview notice pauses this module.

Recheck live: business-activity boundary, nationality/ABTC eligibility, current visa charge and processing guide, ImmiAccount prompts, attachment limits, translation wording, China biometric routing, possible health/character requests, and grant conditions. Official examples of evidence are **case-dependent**, not a universal mandatory list. Filename patterns are internal practice, not Home Affairs requirements.
