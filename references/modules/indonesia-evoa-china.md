# Indonesia e-VOA / 印尼电子落地签（中国大陆申请）

Module ID: `indonesia-evoa-china` · Maturity: `M1 OFFICIAL_REVIEWED` for nationality/channel screening, **not** portal-field certification · Verified: 2026-09-26.

Scope: Chinese mainland ordinary-passport tourist applying before departure from China for the e-VOA product shown in the live [official immigration portal](https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/). Check actual product code, purpose, entry point, duration, and current fee in the portal; do not conflate e-VOA, ordinary eVisa, visa on arrival and visa-free entry. Work, long stay, or an individual interview is outside this route.

The [Indonesian Immigration FAQ](https://jakartapusat.imigrasi.go.id/faqs) lists `Tiongkok` among e-VOA nationalities. It states an original passport valid at least six months and return/onward ticket for e-VOA; its online-visa section lists passport biodata image, photo, email and payment card. Current upload limits, product code, duration, fee and permitted entry points must be checked in the live portal rather than copied from a cached page.

Use the [shared nine gates](../online-visitor-visa-workflow.md). Build an evidence matrix for passport, photo, onward travel, tourist purpose, arrival details and portal-specific prompts. Reconcile identity and dates, check file format/size from the live UI, review the final draft and any non-refundable charge, then request immediate confirmation separately for upload, submission and payment. Track the result, compare the visa to the passport, and prepare the applicant for border inspection. No sandbox action may create an account or charge a card.

`BLOCKER`: wrong product/purpose, passport expiry, inconsistent identity or no required onward evidence. `WARNING`: cached nationality/entry-point lists and actual portal eligibility can diverge; stop if the live selector conflicts with the FAQ. `ADVISORY`: internal filename format is not a government naming mandate.

## 中文执行摘要

印尼官方移民问答将中国列为电子落地签适用国之一，但实际案件仍要在官方门户确认当期 B1 产品、旅游用途、入境口岸、停留期限与费用，不能混用电子落地签、普通电子签和免签。核对至少六个月有效的护照、返程或续程票、照片及实时表单要求；遇到门户与旧问答不一致时暂停。上传、提交、付款分别取得即时同意，沙盒不创建真实账号或扣款。
