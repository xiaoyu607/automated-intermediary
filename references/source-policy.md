# Source and freshness policy / 来源与时效策略

Use this reference whenever eligibility, documents, fees, dates, procedures, or legal/administrative requirements affect a live case.

## Source hierarchy

1. Binding or primary sources: law, regulation, official government, embassy, consulate, university, regulator, or issuing authority.
2. Official operational sources: the authority's application portal, authorised service provider, help centre, form instructions, or appointment system.
3. Secondary professional sources: recognised professional bodies or regulated advisers.
4. Community reports: forums, social posts, videos, applicant reports, and intermediary articles.

Use level 4 to discover possible pitfalls, never as the sole basis for a binding requirement. When level 1 and level 2 appear to conflict, identify the scope and date of each; do not silently choose one.

## Record for every material rule

- claim or requirement;
- source URL and page title;
- authority and source level;
- jurisdiction, category, applicant profile, and application location to which it applies;
- source update date when shown;
- verification date;
- next review date;
- exact workflow rule or test affected.

## Freshness

Always refresh immediately before a live submission. During active maintenance, review volatile items such as fees, portals, forms, appointments, and processing times more frequently than stable workflow structure. A changed page hash or date is a review trigger, not proof that the rule changed.

## Claims

Label information as one of:

- `REQUIRED`: supported by an applicable first-party rule.
- `OFFICIAL_GUIDANCE`: first-party operating guidance or recommendation.
- `INTERNAL_PRACTICE`: this workflow's convention for reliability or clarity.
- `COMMUNITY_SIGNAL`: an unverified lead that requires confirmation.
- `UNKNOWN`: material fact not yet verified.

Never promote a lower-confidence label without new evidence.
