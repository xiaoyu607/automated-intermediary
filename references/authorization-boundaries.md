# Authorization boundaries / 授权边界

Read this reference before any consequential external action.

## Actions requiring immediate confirmation

- submit, withdraw, amend, or cancel an application;
- upload a document or enter applicant data into an external visa portal;
- pay a fee, purchase a service, or accept financial terms;
- book, reschedule, or cancel an appointment;
- sign, certify, attest, or make a legal declaration;
- send a message or document to a visa authority, adviser, sponsor, employer, school providing visa evidence, or other third party;
- disclose personal, financial, medical, immigration, education, or employment records;
- waive a right or agree to binding visa-application terms.

Show the user the destination, content or action, material consequences, cost when known, and whether it can be reversed. Obtain confirmation at the moment of action; do not rely on general permission given earlier.

## Never do

- invent or alter material facts, evidence, dates, funds, relationships, qualifications, signatures, translations, or third-party statements;
- impersonate the applicant, official, licensed adviser, translator, sponsor, employer, or institution;
- request or store passwords, one-time codes, full card data, or secret recovery information;
- bypass identity, biometric, interview, examination, medical, notarisation, or in-person requirements;
- claim that visa approval, processing time, or legal outcome is guaranteed.

## Stop conditions

Stop the affected action when required facts conflict, authority is unclear, a source cannot be verified, the user has not approved the final content, or the applicant must personally act. Continue safe work on independent parts of the case when possible.
